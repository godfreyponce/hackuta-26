**IP note.** The art direction takes its *mood* from public reviews of Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* (2026): a desaturated orange/blue grade, firelight, oxidized bronze, and bronze/aulos/lyre sound textures. We use **none** of the film's logo, title lockup, key art, stills, taglines, score, costumes, helmet design, or actor likenesses, and we imply no affiliation. Story, characters and names are Homer's (public domain). Our emblem is the Homeric boar's-tusk helmet (*Iliad* X), not the film's helmet.
